An update, step by step

1Job

A job arrives from AWS IoT over MQTT and starts if it is newer. Golden 1.5.0 still waits for Button 1.

2Download

The application fetches the image over HTTPS on Wi-Fi into the secondary slot and checks its SHA-256.

3Verify and swap

After a reset MCUboot checks the signature and the version, and swaps the slots.

4Trial

The new image must reconnect to AWS IoT within 5 minutes. Only then does it confirm itself.

5Otherwise

The watchdog or the 5-minute limit resets the board and MCUboot puts the previous image back.

Serial recoveryno network, no debugger

Hold Button 1 during a reset, send a command in the half second after a reset, or have no valid image left: MCUboot stays and takes an image over UART from tools/recover.py. It refuses a wrong signature at the restart. There is no version check and no trial on this path. Tested on the board: a command after a reset (--reset), --console, and no valid image. Not tested on hardware yet: Button 1.

Sizes are from the 1.14.0 build for the nRF7002 DK. In that build the signed image of TF-M and the application is 876,412 bytes, in a 933,888-byte slot. Bars are not to scale. A revert skips the version check: see firmware/README.md. The vault is TF-M’s storage for the device key, certificates and Wi-Fi credentials. The network core is flashed over the debug port and is not updated over the air.
