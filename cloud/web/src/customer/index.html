In development

Your DJ controller, without the laptop.

Cranium is an attachment for beginner DJ controllers such as the Pioneer FLX4. With Bluetooth audio and a Raspberry Pi, the controller is meant to run on its own, with no computer at any point.

Sign in How it works

Cranium is being built by a student team at Penn. Accounts are made by the team for the people it gives a board to: there is no sign-up.

Side A · Good things on repeat

Your account

Your deck, a little more connected.

Sign in and you see every Cranium on your account, as it last reported. Each board reports every 30 seconds while it is on.

  • Whether it is online

    Online, offline, restarting or updating, and when it was last heard from.

  • Which firmware it runs

    The version on each board, and whether a new one is still on trial.

  • How its updates went

    An update as it downloads and installs, and how the last one ended.

  • What it did lately

    When it came online or went offline, restarted, or changed firmware.

Updates

Updates that look after themselves.

Cranium sends firmware updates over the air, and a board takes only what was signed. There is nothing to do on your side.

  1. Signed. Every update carries a signature that only Cranium can make.
  2. Checked. The board checks it before it installs anything.
  3. On trial. A new update has 5 minutes to reach Cranium.
  4. Kept, or undone. If it cannot, the board goes back to the firmware it had, by itself.
